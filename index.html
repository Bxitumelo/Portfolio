My 5 step process

From the first brief to the final mock-up, every project follows the same five steps.

1

Brief

  • Creative brief and contextual breakdown
  • Will it be print or digital?
2

Research

  • Compile moodboard
  • Highlight key words
  • Establish visual direction
3

Concept design

  • Layout design
  • Rough sketches
  • Typography experimentation
4

Refinement

  • Digitize strong concepts
  • Apply suitable colours and fonts
  • Revise mistakes
5

Final delivery

  • Deliver final design
  • Create mock-ups for presentation (if needed)
